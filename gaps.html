Coverage Gaps

Where does the index not yet reach? This page lists, region by region, the UN member states with at least one indexed oral-history or voice-testimony collection — and those with none yet.

This shows gaps in the indexed dataset, not in oral history. The absence of a pin is not evidence of absence: every country listed here as “not yet indexed” has its own oral traditions, and very likely archives that record them, which have not yet been found or verified.

Know a collection in one of these countries? Propose it — no Git or JSON needed. It must be a real, publicly documented collection with its own page (inclusion criteria, contributing guide).

Counts describe the collections indexed in Mapping Voices, placed by the country each collection's pin belongs to (Methodology §5). The list of 193 member states and their UN M49 regions is a static reference file, un-m49-countries.json. Territories and other non-member entities are shown where indexed but not counted as gaps; grouping follows UN M49 and records no political claim.
