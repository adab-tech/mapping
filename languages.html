This view measures discoverable, indexed archival representation — not the existence or importance of any language's oral traditions. A language with one collection here may have rich oral archives that are not yet indexed.

Languages are recorded as the sources state them and are never inferred from geography. Group terms (such as “Multiple languages”) are used when a source names only a group; the source's wording is kept on each collection. Classification rules: Methodology §6.
