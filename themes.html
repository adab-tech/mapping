Themes are discovery aids, not exhaustive descriptions. Each is assigned from what the sources say a collection is principally about; a collection of life-history interviews touches many more subjects than its themes record.

Counts describe the collections indexed in Mapping Voices only — not how much oral history exists on any subject. A group's count is the number of distinct collections with at least one of its themes, so it can be lower than the sum of its themes. Assignment rules: Methodology §7.
